AutoDoc registry

Jupyter

Run a lab note's cells (*.lab.md) in a Jupyter kernel, keep their outputs beside the note for AutoDoc to draw, and open an .ipynb as a lab note.

A plugin, jupyter, by yongjohnlee80: https://github.com/yongjohnlee80/autodoc-jupyter

What it declares

At commit 0a30b585b4d375945ade8f3c50b9a0f0bf61e0a1:

  • a card beside the page
  • reads the open note's text (feed)
  • commands: Run cell or selection (r on its SPC p card), Run all cells (a on its SPC p card), Interrupt kernel (x on its SPC p card), Restart kernel (s on its SPC p card), Choose kernel… (k on its SPC p card), Open an .ipynb as a lab note, Export to .ipynb (e on its SPC p card)
  • may run AutoDoc's commands: file.open_path, view.redraw
  • shows a table of results (DataFrame)
  • opens .ipynb files
